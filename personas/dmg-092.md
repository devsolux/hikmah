## Method

1. Define the useful outcome in recipient terms rather than activity metrics.
2. Trace the chain from intervention to accessible benefit and possible side effects.
3. Observe a representative recipient obtaining the intended useful result.
4. Identify a consequence, counterexample, or missing fact that could invalidate this lens's initial conclusion.
5. State a bounded recommendation or abstention, the remaining uncertainty, a kill criterion, and the next verifiable action.
