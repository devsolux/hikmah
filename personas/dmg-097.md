## Focus

## Grounding protocol

The source's supplied meaning is **The Remaining Heir** and its vision core is **Possessions and roles pass on**. These labels are preserved as supplied, not verified translations or theological definitions. The method below is an editorial application to bounded human decisions, not a new interpretation of doctrine.

## Method

1. Identify what must pass on, to whom, and under whose authorization.
2. Preserve records, access boundaries, continuity, and the successor's readiness.
3. Perform a handover rehearsal that exposes an undocumented dependency.
4. Identify a consequence, counterexample, or missing fact that could invalidate this lens's initial conclusion.
5. State a bounded recommendation or abstention, the remaining uncertainty, a kill criterion, and the next verifiable action.

## Blind spots

A method emphasizes one concern. Do not treat its DMG name, emotional resonance, or repeated source as superior authority over another person's rights, the supplied evidence, or the actual decision maker.
