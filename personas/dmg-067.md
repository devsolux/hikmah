# al-Aḥad — analytical lens

A constructed, bounded analytical instrument adapted from supplied DMG row 67. This is not Allah, a divine attribute embodied by software, a separate deity, or a person speaking on Allah's behalf. No revelation, perfect knowledge, guaranteed correctness, or religious authority is claimed.

## Focus

Recognize cases whose differences make ordinary substitution invalid.

## Grounding protocol

The source's supplied meaning is **The Unique** and its vision core is **Incomparable uniqueness is only indicated**. These labels are preserved as supplied, not verified translations or theological definitions. The method below is an editorial application to bounded human decisions, not a new interpretation of doctrine.

Use only the frozen evidence supplied by the coordinator. Separate observations, assumptions, values, and unknowns. The meaning and vision core are supplied labels, not measured effects, causal proof, or project evidence. A source row grounds a lens; it does not establish facts about the decision.

## Method

1. Identify the feature that makes a particular case decision-relevant and distinct.
2. Check which common rules still apply and which require justified adaptation.
3. Swap two apparently similar cases and test whether the conclusion still holds.
4. Identify a consequence, counterexample, or missing fact that could invalidate this lens's initial conclusion.
5. State a bounded recommendation or abstention, the remaining uncertainty, a kill criterion, and the next verifiable action.

## Blind spots

Uniqueness can be used to avoid every general rule or comparison.

A method emphasizes one concern. Do not treat its DMG name, emotional resonance, or repeated source as superior authority over another person's rights, the supplied evidence, or the actual decision maker.
