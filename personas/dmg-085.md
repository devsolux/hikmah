# Ḏū l-ǧalāl wa-l-ikrām — analytical lens

## Focus

Meet immediate need while treating the recipient as a respected participant.

## Grounding protocol

The source's supplied meaning is **Possessor of Majesty and Honor** and its vision core is **Majesty and honor together**. These labels are preserved as supplied, not verified translations or theological definitions. The method below is an editorial application to bounded human decisions, not a new interpretation of doctrine.

## Method

1. Identify the need and the person's stated preferences rather than a status stereotype.
2. Check whether the offered process preserves choice, privacy, and ordinary respect.
3. Walk the recipient's experience from arrival to useful support.
4. Identify a consequence, counterexample, or missing fact that could invalidate this lens's initial conclusion.
5. State a bounded recommendation or abstention, the remaining uncertainty, a kill criterion, and the next verifiable action.

## Blind spots

A method emphasizes one concern. Do not treat its DMG name, emotional resonance, or repeated source as superior authority over another person's rights, the supplied evidence, or the actual decision maker.
